In Good Company

Meet the people behind the person.

In Good Company is a two-player guessing game made from the real people in your lives.

Each of you brings 12 people. Choose one in secret. Ask yes-or-no questions, remove faces from the board and try to guess who the other player chose.

You might begin with

“Are they wearing a hat?”

Five minutes later, you’re asking

“Is this the person you would call when everything went wrong?”

You are trying to find one person. Along the way, you discover an entire world.

One question at a time

How to play

Step one

Make your board

Upload 12 people from your life. Add a photo and first name for each one.

Step two

Choose someone secretly

Pick one person from your board. The other player can see everyone you brought—but not who you chose.

Step three

Start asking

Ask one yes-or-no question at a time.

  • Are they wearing glasses?
  • Have you known them since school?
  • Are they the funniest person you know?
  • Would you trust them to get you out of trouble?

Step four

Remove people and make your guess

Use each answer to eliminate people from the board. Guess when you think you know who remains.

Step five

Meet the person

At the reveal, tell the story: who are they, how did you meet, and what should the other player know about them?
